Working Principle of the Shredded Machine

Plastic waste is introduced through the hopper. An electric motor supplies rotational motion to the cutting mechanism through a drive connection. The rotating cutters reduce the plastic into smaller fragments, which can then be collected and sent for further recycling or processing.

Assumptions & limitations

  • Proportions, colours and layout follow the top-down photo of the prototype. Board, hopper, motor, clamp brackets, threaded rod, switches, wires and pulley are modelled from what is visible.
  • Illustrative parts: the internal cutter layout (two counter-rotating interlocking sets with end gears), the belt drive from motor pulley to cutter shaft, the discharge chute, the collection tray and the plastic pieces are not visible in the photo.
  • Particle motion is a visual simulation, not a physics calculation. Pieces do not collide with each other precisely.
  • Displayed RPM, power and counts are simulated. On-screen spinning is scaled down so rotation stays visible on a 60 Hz display.
